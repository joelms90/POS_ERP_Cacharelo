# Quickstart: Role Unification Verification

1. Assign canonical roles and verify dashboard/POS access.
2. As branch operator, sell, request stock, then confirm self-approval is denied.
3. As warehouse/purchasing operator, approve the requisition and dispatch its transfer.
4. As branch operator, partially receive transfer and verify a note is required.
5. Receive a supplier PO with different quantity/cost and verify audit evidence.
6. Verify legacy `encargado` and `responsable` users enter the branch-operator experience.
